## Concept scope
Two routes: / and /about. No product detail pages or checkout. Three sample tyre ranges, no invented prices or stock assertions. Finder records requested size but does not claim verified vehicle fitment. Enquiry basket is stored locally; preparing an enquiry creates an on-screen brief and a phone link. No enquiry is transmitted or appointment booked.

## Video loop
The original clip is 10 seconds at 24fps. Opening and ending frames were inspected. A 22-frame (0.916667-second) cross-dissolve blends the final pull-away shot into the opening coastal shot. The loop begins at frame 22, runs through the middle of the clip and finishes on the blend back to frame 22. Video is silent, H.264, fast-start, approximately 3.1 MB. The hero includes pause/play and a static poster for reduced-motion preferences.
